Cover: `thumb/thumb.html` rendered to `out/tiktok-cover.png` (1080×1920).

## Caption

3 hospitals. 0 beds. 118 minutes. 💔

In February, 29-year-old Charles Amissah was taken from hospital to hospital in Accra, and every one said "no bed". Ghana has fewer than 1 hospital bed for every 1,000 people, and ambulances still find free beds by driving around.

We're building BedAlert 🇬🇭: a live view of free hospital beds, so ambulances, hospitals and families know where to go before they leave. Works on smartphones AND basic phones (USSD + SMS).

Built by Robert (Biomedical Engineering, KNUST), with Jayne & Welam (Medical Laboratory Science, University of Ghana).

BedAlert build update 🇬🇭🏥

Phase 1 ✅ The core system is built and tested: secure bed database, bed holds that expire, auto re-routing when a hospital declines, hospital API keys, a USSD menu for basic phones, 31 automated tests passing.

Phase 2 🚧 Now building the three apps: hospital portal, dispatch console and family app. Then real SMS + USSD, and a pilot in our first district.
